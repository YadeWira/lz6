# v1.6.14-pre against v1.6.13-pre, levels -9 to -13, per file

Sizes are exact (the 19 files of Silesia and AIT A, B, C, E, F, G, H, AIT D left out); decode and encode costs are exact user-mode cycle counts (perf counters around
the decode loop and around the compress call, `stdh.c`, one pinned core, interleaved rounds). The streams are compared level for level: the fast tier and levels -1 to
-8 are byte-identical in the two versions. The encode cost is measured on 8 files of Silesia (dickens, mr, nci, ooffice, osdb, samba, sao, xml) because the top levels take half an hour per pass of the whole set. Cycle counts move by about
+-1% between passes on a loaded machine, and single-file numbers by more: read a file below +-3% as noise.
